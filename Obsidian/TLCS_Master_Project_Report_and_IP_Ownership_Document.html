5.1 Core Mathematical Formulations

Mathematical Model Formulation & Implementation Quantitative Utility
Central Pivot Range (CPR) Pivot (P) = (High + Low + Close) / 3
Bottom Central (BC) = (High + Low) / 2
Top Central (TC) = (P - BC) + P 		Establishes market balance equilibrium; identifies trend days vs trading range sideways auction profiles.
Normalized Narrow CPR (Breakouts) cprWidth = |TC - BC|
prevRange = max(DH - DL, mintick)
NCPR = (cprWidth / prevRange) * 100 < 5.0% 		Quantifies severe intraday volatility compression, anticipating explosive breakout expansion days.
Camarilla H4/L4 Touch-Points H4 = Close + Range * 1.1 / 2
L4 = Close - Range * 1.1 / 2
Long Gating: Low < H4 | Short Gating: High > L4 		Touch-point limit order gating linking executions strictly to candle wicks; never relies on candle close.
Neutral Zone Scalp Alert Confluence Scalp_Alert = AnySignal and not (Greenzone or Redzone) Prohibits long and short trades inside opposite adverse zones, ensuring clear runway for expansion.
Confirmed Divergences (D & H) Unconfirmed on Bar 0.
Long close: (Close > Open) and (Close > Close[1])
Short close: (Close < Open) and (Close < Close[1]) 		Eliminates false divergence whipsaws by strictly gating entries to subsequent candle confirmation close.

6. DETERMINISTIC RISK MANAGEMENT & TRAILING STOP HIERARCHY

TLCS enforces a rigid, step-level trailing stop loss architecture designed to systematically harvest alpha, lock in accrued gains, and completely eliminate ambiguous floating stop calculations.

FIGURE 6.1: STEP-LEVEL TRAILING STOP ADVANCEMENT STATE MACHINE

6.1 Midpoint Breakeven Trailing SL Mandate

Formal Rule: For any active filled trade, when the price reaches or crosses the 50% midpoint distance between Entry and TP1 before TP1 is triggered:
Midpoint Level = (Entry + TP1) / 2.0
Long Condition: High ≥ Midpoint Level &implies; Stop Loss ← Entry (Breakeven)
Short Condition: Low ≤ Midpoint Level &implies; Stop Loss ← Entry (Breakeven)
Eliminates round-trip losers and guarantees that any trade achieving 50% of its initial target risk cannot degenerate into a capital loss.

6.2 Canonical Win Rate & Success Rate Formula

Across all components, tabs, and analytics teardowns, TLCS strictly enforces the inclusion of realized Breakeven trades in the closed trade denominator:
Win Rate % = (Total Wins / Total Closed Trades (Wins + Losses + Breakevens)) * 100
The conventional practice of excluding breakevens (Wins / (Wins + Losses)) is strictly prohibited as misleading and artificially inflated.
