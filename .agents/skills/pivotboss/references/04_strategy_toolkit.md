# Step 3: Strategy Toolkit (PDF pp. 51–110)

The Strategy Toolkit categorizes trading strategies by relative risk profiles, allowing traders to match the strategy risk to the opportunity's probability of profit.

## Risk Spectrum
- **Low Risk:** Credit Spreads (Put Credit Spread, Call Credit Spread)
- **Moderate Risk:** Debit Spreads (Call Debit Spread, Put Debit Spread)
- **Neutral / Volatility:** Long Straddle (Breakout), Short Iron Butterfly (Range)
- **High Risk / Aggressive:** ITM Options (Delta 70+), OTM Options ("Lottery Tickets")
- **Underlying & Hedging:** Covered Call (Income), Protective Put (Hedge), Collar (Hedge)

---

## 3.1 Credit Spreads (pp. 54–62)
- **Net Credit:** Selling higher-priced option, buying lower-priced option for protection.
- **Defined Risk:** Max loss = strike difference - net credit.
- **Time Decay:** Works in trader's favor. Trade with **15 days or less to expiration**.
- **Max Profit:** Retain net credit if options expire worthless. Take windfall profits if achieved early.
- **Put Credit Spread (Bullish):** Short Put (higher strike), Long Put (lower strike). Breakeven = higher strike - net credit.
- **Call Credit Spread (Bearish):** Short Call (lower strike), Long Call (higher strike). Breakeven = lower strike + net credit.

---

## 3.2 Debit Spreads (pp. 63–71)
- **Net Debit:** Buying higher-priced option, selling lower-priced option to offset decay and reduce cost basis.
- **Defined Risk:** Max loss = debit paid. Max profit = strike difference - debit paid.
- **Call Debit Spread (Bullish):** Long Call (lower strike), Short Call (higher strike). Breakeven = lower strike + net debit.
- **Put Debit Spread (Bearish):** Long Put (higher strike), Short Put (lower strike). Breakeven = higher strike - net debit.

---

## 3.3 Neutral Strategies (pp. 72–81)
- **Long Straddle (Breakout):** Long Call + Long Put (same strike & expiration). Used when IV is very low and explosive breakout is anticipated. Unlimited reward, capped risk (debit paid). Buy 45+ DTE, exit 20-30 DTE if no expansion.
- **Short Iron Butterfly (Range):** Short ATM Call + Short ATM Put + Long OTM Call + Long OTM Put. Used when IV is high and price is anticipated to compress. Max profit = credit received. Trade with ≤ 15-30 DTE.

---

## 3.4 In-The-Money (ITM) Options (pp. 82–90)
- **Asset Legs:** Delta 70 or higher.
- **When to Use:** High conviction / aggressive directional bets.
- **Characteristics:** Intrinsic + time value. Capped risk (premium paid), uncapped reward. 30-45 DTE.

---

## 3.5 Out-of-The-Money (OTM) Options (pp. 91–99)
- **Asset Legs:** Delta 30 or lower ("Lottery Tickets").
- **When to Use:** Extremely bullish/bearish with rapid expected move.
- **Characteristics:** Cheap, high percentage gain potential, but high probability of total loss due to time decay. 45+ DTE.

---

## 3.6 Trade the Underlying & Hedging (pp. 100–110)
- **Covered Call (Income):** Long stock + Short OTM Call. Generates income on held positions.
- **Protective Put (Hedge):** Long stock + Long OTM/ATM Put. Superior to stop orders ahead of earnings.
- **Collar (Hedge):** Long stock + Long OTM Put + Short OTM Call. Zero/low-cost hedge.
