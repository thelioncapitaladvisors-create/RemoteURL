# Step 4: Execution & Scaling Techniques (PDF pp. 111–125)

Execution error is the most costly of all trading errors. Practice flawless execution.

## Scaling Into a Position (Scale-In)

### 4.1 1x3 Entry Technique (pp. 113–114)
- **Concept:** Splits maximum allowable trade allocation into thirds (33% each).
- **1st Entry (33%):** Last hour of confirmed Rejection Day (or FNL/OD).
- **2nd Entry (33%):** Retest of Absorption Zone / MID on Day 2 or 3.
- **3rd Entry (33%):** Defend position if Failed New Low develops on Days 3-4.
- **Variation:** 50% on 1st entry, 25% on 2nd entry, 25% on 3rd entry.
- **Benefit:** Test with small risk; reward winning setups with additional size.

### 4.2 1x2 Entry Technique (pp. 115–116)
- **Concept:** Splits allowable trade allocation into halves (50% each).
- **1st Entry (50%):** Last hour of Rejection Day or at/near Absorption Zone.
- **2nd Entry (50%):** Reserve last 50% to defend position at Absorption Zone / FNL.

### 4.3 100% Entry Technique (p. 117)
- **Concept:** Enter full 100% trade allocation at once. Reserved for highest odds / best setups.

## Scaling Out of a Position (Scale-Out / Profit-Taking)

### 4.4 11 Scaling Technique (pp. 119–120)
- **Concept:** Scaling out in 2 equal parts (50% each).
- **1st Scale (50%):** Target 1 (T1) — first expansion day / major pop in your favor. Pay yourself early.
- **2nd Scale (50%):** Target 2 (T2) — forecasted target zone.

### 4.5 111 Scaling Technique (pp. 121–122)
- **Concept:** Scaling out in 3 equal parts (33% each).
- **1st Scale (33%):** T1 — end of first breakout day after absorption.
- **2nd Scale (33%):** T2 — entrance into forecasted target zone.
- **3rd Scale (33%):** T3 — inside target zone after failure to hold yHI, completing trade.
- **Add-Back Option:** Add back 1/3 scale upon retest of secondary absorption zone.

### 4.6 211 Scaling Technique (pp. 123–124)
- **Concept:** Scale 50% first, then two 25% scales.
- **1st Scale (50%):** End of first breakout day.
- **2nd Scale (25%):** Retest of recent highs / resistance.
- **3rd Scale (25%):** Final runner pop into major target.
