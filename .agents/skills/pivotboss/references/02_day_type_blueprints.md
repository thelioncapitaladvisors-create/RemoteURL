# Step 2a: Day Type Blueprints (PDF pp. 16–31)

All blueprints below are written **bullish**. The PDF says "*Flip for bearish blueprint*". Mirror every condition (low↔high, above↔below, upper↔lower).

Notation: `H, L, O, C` = today; `yHI, yLO, yMID` = previous session; `MID = (H+L)/2`; `MID[1]` = previous day's MID; `ADR` = average daily range (10-day).

---

## 1. Rejection Day Blueprint (p.17–21)
**Background**
- Forms at price extremes (previous highs/lows).
- Range is much larger than the 10-day average.
- Ideal swing entry is the Rejection Day midpoint. It can be defended for 1–4 days after the rejection.
- A daily close below the MID breaks the pattern, because it signals a retest of the Rejection Day low.
- Usually followed by Absorption Days, which fuel the reversal.

**Measurements**
1. RANGE > 125% ADR
2. TAIL > ideally 2.5 × BODY
3. CLOSE usually in the upper 35% of the range
4. MID = (H+L)/2 is the ideal swing entry. Price must stay above it for the sequence to remain intact.
5. LOCATION: strongest at market structure, previous lows, and low-volume nodes (LVN/CLVN)
6. ENTRIES: ideal entry is MID on Day 1, or just before the close on the rejection day. On Day 2+, **price must OPEN > MID[1]** for an entry at MID to be considered.

**Entries**
- **Entry 1:** Rejection MID down to the rejected low. Defend 1–4 days unless a daily close occurs below MID.
- **Entry 2:** Retest of the Rejection Day high/close from above. Defend 1–2 days.

**Keys to good rejection days (p.21):** a significant rejection at a significant level; price holds the absorption zone on Day 2 with minimal adverse excursion; fast profits, with price rising day after day and no stalling.

## 2. Absorption Day Blueprint (p.19–20)
**Background**
- Forms after significant rejection or expansion days.
- Small range, which allows two-way trade. "Bets are being placed" ahead of the next move.
- Can be one day or a series of days.
- **Failed absorption usually leads to significant stop run days.**

**Measurements**
1. RANGE < 75% ADR
2. CLOSE > rejection/expansion day midpoint, and usually > OPEN
3. LOW near the rejection/expansion day midpoint
4. LOCATION: after rejection or expansion days. Often an **Inside Day**.
5. ENTRIES: ideal entry is yMID (the rejection day's MID), but **price must OPEN > yMID** for an entry to be considered. Several rotations into the trigger zone can happen.

**Entries**
- **Entry 1:** Rejection Day MID. Defend 1–4 days after the rejection unless a daily close occurs below it.
- **Entry 2:** Rejection of the Absorption Day's low on the next session.

**Note (p.20):** When entering on an absorption day at the MID, the bar will look incomplete and "bearish" at entry time, but ideally closes higher. A daily close below the zone tends to cause long liquidation.

## 3. Failed New Low Blueprint (p.22–25)
**Background**
- Starts with strong rejection at the previous day's low or at multi-day lows.
- After the rejection, the previous low (the rejected level) becomes the **primary absorption zone**.
- Ideally closes above the previous session's midpoint.
- Can trigger reversals or fuel trend continuation. Often a trap that adds fuel to the existing trend.

**Measurements**
1. RANGE = **75–100% ADR** (average range)
2. LOW < previous session low, sometimes below multi-day lows
3. CLOSE > previous session MID (ideal case)
4. LOCATION: strongest with previous highs/lows and CLVNs. Can also fuel continuation inside an existing trend.
5. ENTRIES: ideal day-trade entry is yLO on Day 1, and the close for swing trades. On Day 2, **price must OPEN > MID[1]** for an entry at the FNL midpoint.

**Entries**
- **Entry 1:** Day trade: watch for failed range expansion below previous lows, then defend the previous low from above.
- **Entry 1B:** Partial or full entry before the close of the rejection day, if price closes above yMID.
- **Entry 2:** Swing: defend the FNL midpoint 1–2 days after the rejection unless a daily close occurs below it.

**High-odds note (p.25):** An FNL after **range compression** can produce explosive moves.

## 4. Outside Day Blueprint (p.26–29)
**Background**
- Forms at price extremes.
- Range larger than average, so the day is statistically significant.
- Ideal swing entry is yLO; the Outside Day MID is also valid.
- A daily close below the OD MID breaks the pattern, because it signals a retest of the OD low.

**Measurements**
1. RANGE > 105% ADR
2. LOW < previous session low, forcefully rejected
3. CLOSE > previous session high (this completes the outside-day rejection)
4. MID = (H+L)/2 is the ideal swing entry. Price must stay above it.
5. LOCATION: market structure, previous lows, LVNs
6. ENTRIES: ideal entry is yLO on Day 1, or just before the close. On Day 2+, **price must OPEN > MID[1]**.

**Entries**
- **Entry 1:** Between the OD MID and the rejected level (usually the previous low). Defend 1–4 days unless a daily close occurs below this zone. *(Primary Absorption Zone)*
- **Entry 2:** Retest of the previous high down to the OD close. Defend 1–2 days. *(Secondary Absorption Zone)*
- **Entry 3 (p.28):** Price rejects yLO at the secondary absorption zone.

## 5. Stop Run Day Blueprint (p.30–31)
**Background**
- A price-discovery phase that trends aggressively. Often the biggest days in the market (range 200–300% of average).
- **Target:** take today's range below yHI and project it above yHI → `Target = yHI + (yHI − L)`.
- The day after a Stop Run is usually a countertrend fade day, with price returning to the SRD midpoint.
- Rejection and Absorption days fuel Stop Run days.

**Measurements**
1. RANGE > 200% ADR
2. LOW often coincides with a rejection (failed range expansion)
3. CLOSE > previous session high (longs), usually in the upper 10–15% of the range
4. MID ≥ recent multiple-day highs/resistance (longs)
5. LOCATION: usually after rejection and/or absorption. Strongest with CLVNs.
6. ENTRIES: ideal entry is yLO on Day 1. On Day 2, **price must OPEN > MID[1]** for an entry at yHI/MID[1].

**Entries**
- **Entry 1:** Failed range expansion at yLO. Defend yLO from above after the rejection.
- **Entry 2:** Acceptance above previous highs triggers the stop run. Defend retests of yHI from above. Day trade, or 1–2 day swing unless a daily close occurs below it.

**Trade management (p.31)**
- Confirm the SRD early, intraday if possible, and be more aggressive once confirmed.
- If the SRD closes in the **upper 20%** of its range, Day 2 offers a 2nd entry on a retest of the absorption zone.
- The absorption zone stays bid until a daily close occurs below it.
- At the target: take full profit on a day trade, or **half** on a swing (with the plan to add back the next day).
