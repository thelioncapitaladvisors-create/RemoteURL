# Step 5: Documentation & Trade Review (PDF pp. 126–134)

Documentation and periodic review are vital routines for serious traders to build strengths and eliminate recurring errors.

## 5.1 Entry Documentation (p. 128)
Document immediately at the outset of each trade (via chart screenshot & structured log):
1. **Trade Allocation:** Chosen risk amount ($ and % of core equity).
2. **Setup:** Day Type Blueprint or Trade Sequence (e.g., Rejection Day, FNL, Stop Run).
3. **Strategy:** Options strategy or underlying asset type.
4. **Entry Allocation:** Scale-in technique (1x3, 1x2, or 100%).
5. **Targets:** Forecasted profit targets (T1, T2, T3).
6. **Entry Price:** Exact execution price and option chain snapshot.
7. **Trade Notes:** Confluence zones, market structure, catalysts, general trade plan.

---

## 5.2 Exit Documentation (p. 130)
Document immediately after trade closure:
1. **Exit Price:** Price(s) for the trade across all scale-out stages.
2. **PNL:** Realized absolute profit/loss.
3. **Percent ROI / Exact Pct:** Percentage gain/loss based on exact math `((Exit - Entry) / Entry) * 100`.
4. **Days in Trade:** Duration of exposure.
5. **Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE):** Farthest price moved against entry before exit.
6. **Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE):** Farthest price moved in favor before exit.
7. **Post-Mortem Notes:** What went well, what could improve, unexpected market conditions.

---

## 5.3 Review Cadence (p. 132)
- **Daily Review:** Review today's executions and emotional discipline.
- **Weekly Review:** Analyze win rate, profit factor, sequence completion, and missed opportunities.
- **Monthly Review:** Review market structure shifts, equity curve growth, and portfolio heat.
- **Milestone Review:** Thorough statistical analysis every 100 closed trades.
